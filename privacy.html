Privacy Policy

Effective 31 July 2026

The short version

SleepGate does not require an account, does not show advertising, and does not use analytics. Sleep-related information is processed and stored locally on your device. SleepGate does not upload that information to Ronit Parikh or to a SleepGate-controlled server.

1. Who operates SleepGate

SleepGate is an Android application developed and operated by Ronit Parikh (“SleepGate”, “we”, “us” or “our”). For privacy questions, contact ronitparikh15@gmail.com.

2. Information used on your device

To provide its core bedtime-protection and sleep-estimation features, SleepGate may access and process the following information locally:

  • Sleep preferences and history: bedtime schedules, wake targets, sleep goals, locally estimated sleep intervals, session history and phone-check interruptions.
  • Screen and device state: whether the device is interactive, locked or unlocked, and timestamps needed to estimate eligible sleep time.
  • Sleep and motion signals: sleep-confidence or classification information supplied through Google Play services, plus motion or accelerometer signals used to support the estimate.
  • Protected-app information: the apps you choose to restrict and the identity of the foreground app when needed to enforce blocking.
  • Permission and service status: whether required Android permissions, Accessibility access and foreground services are available.

This information is used only to operate features that you request in SleepGate.

3. Accessibility Service

SleepGate uses Android Accessibility Service access to detect when a user-selected protected app is opened and to display the blocking experience during an active protection session. This is a core app-blocking function. SleepGate is not an accessibility tool.

SleepGate is not designed to read, store or transmit passwords, messages, typed text, screen contents, screenshots or financial information. Accessibility-derived app activity is processed on the device and is not sent to us.

4. Google Play services and Sleep API

SleepGate may receive sleep-classification signals from Google Play services. SleepGate processes the returned signals locally and does not send them to a SleepGate-controlled server. Google Play services is provided by Google and may process device information under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. You can review the Google Privacy Policy.

SleepGate’s results are estimates. A sleep-confidence value is not a medical measurement and does not identify clinical sleep stages such as deep sleep.

5. What we collect, transmit or share

Ronit Parikh and SleepGate do not receive, collect, upload, sell, rent or share your sleep history, device-state information, protected-app activity or motion data. SleepGate does not use advertising, analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs.

If you voluntarily email us for support, we receive the email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it only to respond to your request and do not sell it.

6. Local storage, retention and deletion

SleepGate settings and session information remain in the app’s local storage on your device until you delete them. You can delete locally stored data using SleepGate’s reset/delete control, if available, or through Android Settings > Apps > SleepGate > Storage > Clear storage. Uninstalling SleepGate normally removes its local app data, subject to Android device backup or restore settings that you control.

Because SleepGate has no user accounts and does not store this information on our servers, there is no remote account or cloud sleep record for us to delete.

7. Security

Keeping information on-device reduces remote exposure, but no device or software can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and keeping Android security updates current.

8. Children

SleepGate is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no account or remote data collection, we generally cannot identify the age of a user.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if SleepGate’s features, data practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. If a change materially affects how sensitive information is used, SleepGate will provide any additional notice or consent required by law or platform policy.
